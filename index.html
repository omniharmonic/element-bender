Nature’s response.

Choose an element, then hold and move through the scene. Begin slowly. Let it answer.

With your hands

Enable your camera and step back until both hands are visible. Sweep an open palm to move. Pinch or close your hand to gather. Bring two hands together to condense; spread them wide to disperse.

Circle your hand to swirl. Turn your palm up to lift, down to settle. Your wrists also work when you step farther back. Choose a gesture below for a steady override.

With a mouse or touch

Hold and drag to bend. Select a gesture below for a different force. Right-drag or hold Shift to push. On a touchscreen, use two fingers for two sources of force.

On earth, Flow raises land and Push carves valleys. Streams find new paths and forests follow the terrain. Alt-drag to orbit the island; scroll to move closer.
